Equity Derivatives collar strategy simulation

I develop a pricing model of a protective collar strategy [selling call options to fund put options] on an underlying equity position simulated using Geometric Brownian Motion price paths in Python. I compare the daily mark-to-market returns and maturity payoffs of the underlying alone against those of the underlying combined with the collar.

SG Trend Index convexity study

I look at the convexity of the SG Trend Index relative to the S&P 500 across different return horizons. Convexity seems to peak over 80–90-day periods, probably reflecting the lookback period of the underlying trend-following strategies and highlighting the importance of time-period selection when evaluating their effectiveness.
